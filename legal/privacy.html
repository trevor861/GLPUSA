How GLPUSA collects, uses and protects your personal and health information.

Information collected in connection with your care may include your medical history, intake responses, consultation records, prescriptions, and other protected health information (PHI). PHI is handled according to applicable law and the treating provider’s Notice of Privacy Practices.

The provider’s notice explains how PHI may be used and disclosed for treatment, payment, healthcare operations, and other legally permitted purposes; your rights to inspect and obtain copies of records, request amendments and restrictions, request confidential communications, and obtain an accounting of certain disclosures; and how to exercise those rights or file a privacy complaint. It also explains the provider’s legal duties

Information we collect

Account details, intake responses, medical history you provide, payment information processed by our payment partner, and technical data such as device and usage information.

How we use it

To route your intake to a licensed provider, fulfill prescriptions, provide support, improve the platform and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information with licensed providers, partner pharmacies, payment processors and service providers under contract. We do not sell your personal health information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting support from your account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is limited to personnel who need it to deliver care.
