Testosterone replacement

Injectable Testosterone

Injectable testosterone is one way a clinician may treat confirmed testosterone deficiency. The formulation, schedule, monitoring, and whether treatment is suitable depend on your medical history and test results.

Start assessment Compare options

A prescription is considered only after a licensed clinician reviews your assessment and relevant labs. Pricing and availability are confirmed during review.

A personal decision

What to discuss with your clinician

The right option depends on more than a product format. Your care team needs to understand your symptoms, test results, other health conditions, and future plans.

  • Your symptoms and repeat morning testosterone results
  • Blood counts, blood pressure, and other health considerations
  • Fertility plans before starting testosterone therapy
